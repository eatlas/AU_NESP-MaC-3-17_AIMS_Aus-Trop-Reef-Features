## ATRF-P01-v1-0

### Approximate coverage supporting reef detection

Originally assessed for dataset: v1-0.

Repository version: planned release tag v1-0, pending publication.

Recorded: 4 November 2026.

### Purpose and evidence

This assessment estimates the extent of regions where mapping experience indicated good knowledge of the seabed and reef distribution.

The inputs were approximate author estimates of seabed visibility in satellite imagery and the extent of useful bathymetric coverage. The assessment considered a coastal shelf strip extending roughly 20-50 km offshore, where many coastal reefs occur.

The estimates were prepared manually for the report, rather than generated by A05 or A06.

### Measurement and visibility judgement

Distances were estimated in QGIS using a map of Australia and the Measure tool. The coastline was measured in segments of approximately 50-100 km, using a consistent level of generalisation.

Eric Lawrey selected qualifying sections from his knowledge and recollection of seabed visibility and reef distribution, developed through more than 300 hours of reef digitisation using the imagery.

Examples of qualifying sections included the coast from the Keppel Islands to Hervey Bay, where the seabed could be seen out to the mid-shelf region; inshore waters from Townsville to Mission Beach; and shallow areas near Lizard Island. Other qualifying sections included the southern Gulf of Carpentaria and the coast from the Dampier Peninsula south along the Pilbara to the Houtman Abrolhos. In all of these areas the depth visibility of the imagery was 30 - 40 m.

### Inputs

| Quantity | Approximate value | Interpretation |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Assessed coastline | 8,100 km | Denominator for the coastline-based comparison; the Coral Sea is considered separately. |
| Coastline with clear seabed visibility in satellite imagery | 3,400 km | Sections with useful seabed visibility to depths greater than approximately 20 m in the assessed coastal strip. |
| GBR and Torres Strait bathymetric coverage, expressed as a corresponding coastal length | 2,100 km | High-quality bathymetry, including LADS surveys, resolves much of the offshore reef-bearing shelf despite limited optical visibility in some areas. |
| Clear inshore imagery within the GBR and Torres Strait contribution | 600 km | Already included in the 3,400 km imagery estimate; deducted to avoid double counting in the approximate combination. |

LADS refers to Laser Airborne Depth Sounder surveys. Bathymetric coverage was expressed as an approximate coastal-length equivalent for this comparison.

### Calculation

The imagery-only coastline proportion is:

```text
3,400 / 8,100 = 0.41975, approximately 42%.
```

The combined estimate allows for the overlap between the clear inshore imagery and the GBR and Torres Strait bathymetric contribution:

```text
Combined coastal length = 3,400 + 2,100 - 600 = 4,900 km.
Combined proportion = 4,900 / 8,100 = 0.60494, approximately 60%.
```

The report uses the rounded figures of 42% and 60%.

### Coral Sea

The Coral Sea was assessed separately. Optical visibility reached approximately 40-60 m, with much of the atoll lagoon floor just visible in imagery. Its good coverage was not converted into a coastal-length equivalent or included in the calculation.

### Core limitation

This was an expert assessment of regions understood through reef mapping, not a detailed spatial mapping of seafloor visibility. The percentages describe approximate coastline coverage of those regions, not a measured area of visible seabed.
